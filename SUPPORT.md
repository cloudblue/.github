# Getting help

- **A bug or a feature request for one of our open-source projects:** open an issue in that project's repository.
- **A security issue:** follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md). Please don't open a public issue.
- **Questions about CloudBlue products:** if you are a CloudBlue customer or partner, use your usual support channel. Everyone else can [contact us](https://www.cloudblue.com/contact-us/). Issues in these repositories are not monitored as product support.
- **Using one of our Connect SDKs or tools?** The [Connect developer documentation](https://connect.cloudblue.com/community/) covers the API, extensions and reports.
- **Anything else:** email together@cloudblue.com.
